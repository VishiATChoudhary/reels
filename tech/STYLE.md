# Style teardown: the two reference edits

Measured frame by frame from the full-res downloads (B 1080x1920 at 60 fps, A 2160x3840 at 60 fps).
Sheets at 10 fps in `tech/refs/` (git-ignored). Times are seconds.

## B. Zenith Edits, "Self-pity" (DzYmbIMrZc8, 15.9 s)

### Frame
- Pure black canvas, 1080x1920.
- Footage sits in **one square card, 1052x1052**, at x 14..1065, y 434..1485, dead centre (14 px side margin).
- Corners: **~100 px radius**, continuous (squircle-like, the curve starts ~110 px in).
- No border, no shadow, no glow. The card never moves, scales or animates. Cuts are hard cuts inside the card.
- Footage is cropped to fill the square, subject centred. Very slow drift/push at most; mostly static.

### Captions
- Font: **Helvetica Neue, Medium to Bold (~600)**, all **lowercase**, no punctuation.
- Tracking very tight (about -0.05 em); word space almost closed ("untilsomething", "andnothing").
- White, with a soft dark shadow (~0 2px 12px at 40%) only to lift it off bright footage.
- Position: optical centre of the card (y ~ 960), centred horizontally.
- Size: base ~120 px (x-height ~65 px). A lone strong word ("moved", "active") goes bigger, ~150 px.
- **Stacks**: a small lead word (~55 to 65% size) sits above and left of a big word: "on / everything",
  "like / you / can" (staircase: each line shifts, lines overlap vertically, line-height ~0.78).
  Words are **added** to the stack as spoken; the stack resets on the next phrase.
- **Italic** (same family, bold italic) for one emphasis phrase: "i had".
- Animation: each new word **fades in over ~6 frames at 60 fps (0.1 s)** with a faint ghost/blur, no scale, no slide.
  Words hold until the phrase ends, then the next phrase replaces them on its first word (no fade out).
- 1 to 2 words on screen most of the time, up to 3 in a stack.

### Pacing rules
- 18 shots in 15.9 s, average 0.88 s, range 0.27 to 2.0 s.
- **Cuts land on phrase boundaries**, never mid-word. A shot carries one phrase.
- **Speaker anchor** returns 4 times (0.0, 5.6, 9.7, 13.9); between anchors, 2 to 5 b-roll shots.
- **B-roll is literal**: each shot illustrates its words (happens = runners, sitting there = a burning room, moved = the knight).
- Every b-roll shot is graded to **one dominant colour** (blue, orange, teal, red, grey). Consecutive shots alternate warm and cold.
- Opens and closes on the speaker. No intro card, no outro, no logo.

## A. FEROX, "Pressure" (LqVZ-wT0UbM, 18.2 s)

### Frame
- Black canvas. Footage **full width, 1080x955** (y 482..1437), about 9:8, hard edges, **no rounding**.
- Watermark **FEROX** inside the picture, bottom centre, ~22 px, extended square sans (Eurostile/Michroma style),
  letter-spacing ~0.5 em, white at ~85%.
- Ends: fade to black (16.6 s), then a small white logo mark in the centre for ~1.5 s.

### Captions
- Base words: **Helvetica Neue Bold, lowercase**, smaller than B (~78 px), centred on the picture, white.
- **Italic bold** for verbs and emphasis: *you squeeze*, *your talent*, *deliver*, *sleep*.
- Stacks: a tiny lead (~45%) above the main word: "changes / everything", "some / people", "can you / deliver".
- **Key word cards**: ALL CAPS Helvetica Black, ~170 px, wrapped in quotes (”PRESSURE”, ”THEY FOCUS”).
  The fill is **see-through glass**: the footage shows through the letters, brightened and tinted to the shot's
  colour (teal on teal, skin on skin). Built as white text with an overlay blend over the picture.
- Same fade-in as B (~0.1 s). Captions start only after the opening montage.

### Pacing rules
- Structure: **hook (0.6 s) > silent montage burst (4.2 s) > slow narrative (11 s) > fade out**.
- Burst cuts are 10 to 20 frames each and land on music hits; narrative cuts are 0.6 to 1.6 s and land on phrases.
- The voice opens and closes the piece (bookend).
- Key concepts get the glass caps card; everything else is quiet lowercase.

## What we take for our tech edits
1. Frame: B's square rounded card (1052, r 100) as the default. A's full-width 9:8 as the alternate.
2. Captions: Helvetica Neue lowercase, tight, fade-in 0.1 s, stacks with a small lead word, italics for emphasis.
3. Key words: A's glass caps in quotes (”COMPUTE”), at most 2 per video.
4. Edit: one continuous take; story setup on the build-up, the turning word on the drop, payoff on the beat (../RECIPE.md).
5. B-roll illustrates the exact words; one colour per shot; alternate warm and cold.
6. Watermark: A's style, tiny extended caps, tracked wide, inside the picture bottom: @HANDLE.
7. No HUD, no labels, no glitch, no traces, no terminal. Nothing on screen but footage and words.

## Hook (first 3 s)
Research: viewers decide in ~3 s; text + motion in the first 2 s; hooks must read on mute; picture, voice and
text carry the same hook (tlinky.com/3-second-hook, nestscale.com, virvid.ai).
The approved hook lives **inside one continuous take** (a flash-forward teaser with a hard cut to black and
silence was tried and rejected):
1. Frame 1: the speaker's face, lip-synced, already saying the story's first line; the song's build-up is
   already playing.
2. **Headline** above the card from frame 1 to ~3 s, no fade, a curiosity gap with one italic word:
   "steve jobs found the answer in a study about *birds*".
3. Glass caps on the first words ("”THE CONDOR”"), on screen from frame 1.
