# History theme

Machine settings: `themes/history.json` (song pool, grades, frame, handle). Recipe: `../RECIPE.md`.

| | History |
|---|---|
| Voices | speeches, radio broadcasts, newsreel narration, mission audio, leaders' interviews |
| Story shape | the crisis, then the decision |
| The drop | the line that history remembers ("we choose to go to the **moon**") |
| B-roll | newsreels, archive photos (slow push), maps, the place today, film reconstructions |
| Grades | before the drop `NEWSREEL BW BLEAK SEPIA` (black and white), after `FADED AMBER VIVID RED`: colour arrives on the drop |
| Frame | `card` |
| Music | the epic and soulful Encore beats: STRIKE ME DOWN (choir), SAVIOR, LET ME FALL, UTOPIA |
| Headline | the date and the stakes: "in 1962 a president made a promise nobody could *keep*" |

The black-and-white to colour switch on the drop is this theme's signature: keep every shot before the drop
in `NEWSREEL`, `BW` or `SEPIA`, and use colour footage (or colourised archive) after it.
